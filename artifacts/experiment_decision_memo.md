# Experiment decision memo

> Data mode: **public Hillstrom randomized experiment**.

## Executive decision

The pre-registered primary metric supports the decision to **launch the men's creative for the studied population**. This conclusion applies to customers active in the prior 12 months and the two-week measurement window.

## Experiment validity

- SRM p-value: `0.9037`; SRM detected: `False`.
- Maximum absolute standardized mean difference: `0.016` (rule-of-thumb threshold: 0.10).
- Treatment assignment is analyzed concurrently, not as a before/after comparison.

## Average treatment effects: men's email versus control

- **visit_rate**: 7.66 percentage points; 95% CI [0.0700, 0.0832], BH-adjusted p = 5.117e-111.
- **conversion_rate**: 0.68 percentage points; 95% CI [0.0050, 0.0086], BH-adjusted p = 3.427e-13.
- **revenue_per_user**: $0.770 per user; 95% CI [0.4851, 1.0545], BH-adjusted p = 2.095e-07.

## Power and variance reduction

- visit_rate: detectable absolute lift `0.85 pp` at 80% power.
- conversion_rate: detectable absolute lift `0.22 pp` at 80% power.
- CUPED variance reduction ranged from `0.05%` to `0.06%`. A weak result is reported rather than hidden.

## Heterogeneous treatment effects

- Model selected on validation Qini: **x_learner**.
- Held-out test Qini coefficient: `0.00081`.
- Under the illustrative economics ($5.00 per incremental visit and $0.50 total contact/opportunity cost), validation selects 2% of eligible users; the fixed policy has held-out test net value $44.55.
- The economics are a scenario, not observed profit. Re-estimate them before any production decision.

## Limitations

1. The public experiment is historical email data; the method transfers, but the effect size does not automatically transfer to a content platform.
2. Outcomes cover two weeks and miss delayed behavior.
3. Uplift is modeled on visits because conversion is sparse; conversion and revenue remain business outcomes, not interchangeable labels.
4. Offline policy value requires a prospective randomized holdout before deployment.

## Reproducibility

All metrics were generated by `python scripts/run_pipeline.py`. Raw data and model binaries are excluded from Git; summary tables and figures are versionable.
